Transition Rituals

✦

Transitioning between different mental states requires a bridge. Here are three distinct rituals to help you signal to your psyche that it's time to shift into a deep-focus or quiet-time mode:

The Physical Boundary

Closing a specific door or putting on noise-canceling headphones to signal the shift. It creates a tangible separation between your external environment and your internal workspace.

The Narrative Bridge

"I’m heading into the deep-well now."

A simple phrase used as a vocal cue to provide context for your withdrawal to those around you and to yourself.

The Sensory Anchor

Lighting a specific candle or changing the lighting to mark the start of quiet time. Sensory cues anchor the brain to a specific routine through smell or light temperature.
